# Copy

- Plain, specific and short. Say what happened and what to do: "That code isn't right. Try again."
- Name actions by their outcome ("Save changes", "Send invite"), not by the control ("Submit").
- Sentence case everywhere; counts read "9 invoices, 1 failed".
- No hype, no claims the product can't back. Numbers and dates come from the data, never invented.
- Errors keep what the person typed and offer the next step. Success is said where the change shows, with
  Undo when it can be undone.
